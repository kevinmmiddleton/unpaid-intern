## Patterns across 01-14

- **Session-open noise (07, 08, 09, 10, 13, 14).** The opening line repeats "Due Thu 10/08: revised launch timeline" even though SKILL.md limits session-open mentions to items due today, overdue, or waiting more than five business days. `brain.py due` prints a "Due soon" block, and the model echoes it. Suggested skill fix: add a `brain.py due --session-open` mode that prints only qualifying items, or state in SKILL.md "do not mention due-soon items at session open."
- **Guessed pronouns for Sam (02, 03).** The skill only bans guessed pronouns inside /who. See the case 02 fix.
- **Date math in its head (04, 12).** Mostly correct, but it breaks "Machines do the math", and in 12 it produced a wrong claim.
- **Jargon in user-facing text.** "Files mode" and "Connected mode" labels, plus `[inferred]` and `[stated]` tags in chat replies to non-technical users (01, 03, 08, 12, 13, 14).
- **Em dashes.** None in any reply.
