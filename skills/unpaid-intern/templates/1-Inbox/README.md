# Inbox

Save anything here: transcripts, PDFs, screenshots, exports, notes. Messy is fine. New somewhere? A screenshot of your org chart is one of the most useful things you can drop here.

Your assistant reads these files and never edits them. When you say /sync-kb (or "file my inbox"), the useful facts go to the right project, area, or reference page, and the raw file moves to 5-Archive/processed.

Keep out anything your company says cannot go into AI tools, plus passwords and customer records.
