## How to use it

- Drop anything into **1-Inbox**: transcripts, PDFs, screenshots, exports, notes. If you can save it, your assistant can read it.
- Say /briefing to start the day, /prep before a meeting, /debrief after one, and /close at the end.
- You never have to memorize commands. Say what you want in plain words. Your personal cheat sheet is [Setup/my-commands.md](Setup/my-commands.md).
- Everything lives in plain files you can open and edit yourself. Nothing here is ever deleted; finished things move to 5-Archive.

## What's in this folder

| Folder | What lives there |
|---|---|
| 1-Inbox | Anything you drop in. Raw files are read, never edited, then filed. |
| 2-Projects | One folder per project: where it stands, what was decided, and the sources behind it. |
| 3-Areas | The ongoing parts of your job with no end date, one page each. |
| 4-Reference | The glossary, the people you work with, and clean notes from sources and meetings. |
| 5-Archive | Filed drops and finished projects. |
| Memory | The lists your assistant keeps up to date: project status, follow-ups, decisions, meetings, and a daily log. |
| Setup | Your preferences, what the assistant may do, your connected tools, and your cheat sheet. |
