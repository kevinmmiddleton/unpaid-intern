# Error decoder

Generated by `connect.py export-md` from the scripts' own data. Do not edit by hand; edit the source and re-run.

Find the message that looks most like the one on screen. If two rows fit, the more specific one wins. If nothing fits: disconnect and connect again, check the login works in the tool's own website, then send the IT email with the exact message.

| Looks like | What it means | What to do | Who can fix it | Goes in the IT email |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Your site admin must authorize this app for the site before you can use it."; "Contact your site admin to request access" | Your Atlassian site admin has to approve Claude once. Jira and Confluence ask a site admin to approve a new app the first time anyone in the company connects it. | Send the IT request to whoever administers Jira or Confluence. After one approval, everyone can connect. | Your Jira or Confluence site admin | Yes |
| "Need admin approval. | "AADSTS53003: Access has been blocked by Conditional Access policies." | Your company only allows sign-ins from approved devices or places. Microsoft sign-in rules (Conditional Access) blocked it. Use the fallback meanwhile. | Your IT or identity team | Yes |
| "AADSTS50105: The signed in user is not assigned to a role for the application" | Your company limits this app to certain people. | "AADSTS50076: Due to a configuration change, you must use multi-factor authentication" | Your company wants an extra verification step. | "AADSTS50020: User account from identity provider does not exist in tenant" | That was a personal account, not your work one. | "Access blocked: Claude has not been approved by your organization"; "Error 403: access_denied. | "HTTP 403 Forbidden: IP address is not allowed"; "Your IP address is not on the allowlist" | The tool refused the connection. | "Error: SELF_SIGNED_CERT_IN_CHAIN self signed certificate in certificate chain" | Your company inspects secure traffic, and Claude Code doesn't trust it yet. | "407 Proxy Authentication Required" | Your company network needs a proxy setting. Traffic from your computer has to go through a company proxy. | "401 Unauthorized: invalid_token, the access token has expired" | Your sign-in expired. | "429 Too Many Requests" | You hit the tool's usage limit. | "listen EADDRINUSE: address already in use :::3118" | Another sign-in is already in progress. Claude Code opens a small sign-in helper on your computer, and something else is using it. | Close other sign-in windows, restart Claude Code, and try again. | Nobody; you can fix this one | No |
| "This workspace is in the EU region. Use the EU endpoint." | Your company's account lives in a different region. | "This feature is not available on your plan. Upgrade to Business."; "This feature isn't available on your current Smartsheet plan." | Your plan for that tool doesn't include it. | "bash: python3: command not found" | Scripts can't run here. Code execution is off, or Python isn't installed. | "The search returned nothing, 0 results" | It connected, but found nothing. | "502 Bad Gateway" | The tool itself is having trouble. The error came from the tool's side, not yours. These usually clear up on their own. | Try again in a few minutes. | "Authorization with the MCP server failed"; "OAuth error: access_denied (user denied)"; "The user canceled the authorization" | The sign-in didn't finish. Claude started the sign-in, but it didn't complete. Often a window was closed early, the wrong account was picked, or the sign-in page showed its own error. | Disconnect and connect again, finish every screen, and pick your work account.
