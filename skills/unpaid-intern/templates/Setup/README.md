# Setup

How your assistant is set up for you:

- **preferences.md**: your timezone, working hours, brief length, and voice
- **guardrail-profile.md**: what the assistant may read, write, and send
- **connection-plan.md**: your work tools and how each one is connected
- **my-commands.md**: your personal cheat sheet
- **setup-profile.md**: your setup answers
- **it-request.md**: a blank email to IT; filled-in copies appear next to it
