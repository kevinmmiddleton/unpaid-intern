# What each command unlocks

Generated by `connect.py export-md` from the scripts' own data. Do not edit by hand; edit the source and re-run.

Use this for the tour when scripts can't run: pick the four commands that match what the person asked for help with, say what each one reads for them, and have them try one. Lead with the core eight; mention the rest only when asked.

## The core eight

| Command | What it does | Reads (when connected) | With nothing connected | Try it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| /briefing | What's due, what's on fire, and what changed overnight, in one short read. | email, calendar, team chat, tasks and tickets | Works today from your follow-up list and dropped files. | `/briefing` |
| /prep | Before a meeting: the last decision, the open question, and the right file. | calendar, email, team chat, files, team's docs, tasks and tickets, customer tools | Paste the invite or name the meeting. | `/prep my next meeting` |
| /debrief | Transcript in; notes, decisions, and follow-ups out, filed under the right project. | meeting transcripts, files | Drop or paste the transcript. | `/debrief the last meeting` |
| /who | Who someone is, what they own, who they report to, and what's open between you. | email, team chat, calendar | Works from your people page, meeting notes, and follow-ups. | `/who and a name from your last meeting` |
| /capture | Put an owner and a date on a promise, yours or someone else's. | nothing; your own records | Works fully. | `/capture and the promise, like "I owe my manager the draft by Thursday"` |
| /close | End the day: what moved, what's still open, and tomorrow's first move. | nothing; your own records | Works fully. | `/close` |
| /project-status | A ready-to-paste status table, built from your own records. | tasks and tickets | Works today from your own project records. | `/project-status` |
| /explain | Any acronym, doc, or engineering note, explained like you're new. | nothing; your own records | Works fully. | `/explain (then paste the thing)` |
